# Helmeted rider

The body and 65-bone humanoid rig are derived from Quaternius' **Universal Base
Characters**, free Standard edition, `Superhero_Male_FullBody.gltf`.

- Author: Quaternius, https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
- Download: https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
- License: CC0 1.0. The archive's original notice is `QUATERNIUS-LICENSE.txt`.
- Dunes modifications: remove the face/head mesh, add an opaque full-face helmet,
  visor and trim, recolor the body as a fire suit, gloves and boots, and pose the
  cloned rig in the vehicle at runtime. No source textures are used.

The source mesh/rig and builder are retained under `blender/rider/` in the
repository. The Dunes additions are released under the repository's Unlicense.
