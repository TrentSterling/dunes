## Closing thoughts and next useful pass

The script-to-model pipeline is now real and repeatable. The remaining modeling
challenge is controlling the shapes with stronger reference constraints and
reviewing them from less flattering angles.

For the next pass, agree on the intended aircraft identity and fit its side,
top and front silhouettes to consistent reference views. Record landmark
ratios and cross sections in the specification. Review those proportions before
adding more surface detail. Kestrel currently uses reference-informed authored
shapes; it is not a measured F-16 reconstruction.

The gear has useful inspection articulation, but does not have an engineered
gear-bay/linkage model. Flight physics, LODs, baked AO, a pilot and integration
with Jet Game or Dustline remain separate tasks. Do not call those complete.
